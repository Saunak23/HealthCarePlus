1  Problem Definition

Health checkups are an important part of life. Also as we have all faced, getting a doctor appointment can be tiresome and sometimes frustrating. Our application eliminates that burden. Users can get appointments sitting at their homes through their mobile phones.
Doctor’s Care application enables user to pick their desired date and time for the appointment with the respective doctor. Also a patient can search and pick a doctor according to a particular disease. We had initially thought of integrating online consultation but due to lack of time we were not able to do so. However the future of our application is to provide online consultation as well.

2  Project Overview

Today’s software systems are usually constructed with many software components implemented by different teams of software developers. Doctor’s Care is an android application which provides appointment booking facility to individual consumers. It makes booking appointments hassle-free. Patients can login using their mobile phones, search for doctors according to their needs and book their desired date and time slot for check-up by making an online payment. Similarly, there is an application for doctors to login and see their appointments for the day, verify the patient using a key and start and end a particular session.

3  Existing System

We show a doctor-patient interaction framework based on Android. Its fabulous execution on portable terminals makes it conceivable that patients are able to associated with specialists, take proposals and book arrangements. We propose a unused framework based on android innovation, through that the specialist can oversee his/her arrangements from any place. In expansion to this the understanding who is incapable to go to the clinic can take the arrangement can too book his/her arrangement from a versatile phone inside 3-4 min. Our arrangement is to construct a framework that will offer assistance the needful individuals or each individual who needs to spare their valuable time. Any required data can be provided at the time of establishment. User interface is pretty easy to use as well. There are many existing projects such as medplus, netmeds and practo. These systems are a bit complicated and thus may be difficult to use. Beyond that detailed diagnosis are less useful, since the database is complex.

4  Proposed System

We improved our User Interface to make it more convenient for our users. Also the data flow is not very complex so as to make the response time faster. We have used Firebase database for data storage which is convenient and data flow is faster compared to others. When the patient logins the phone number gets stored. The profile is created automatically and the patient can search and book appointments for a particular doctor at his or her desired date and time slot. The appointment is confirmed after payment. He or she gets notification reminders as well. The doctor can manage his or her appointments through the doctor side application by logging in using his phone number and a password given by the admin while registering in the application. The token number is then verified by the doctor using the app and the session starts. After the session the patient can view his or her appointments in their Order Details. Our future development includes online consultation and online prescriptions as well.
